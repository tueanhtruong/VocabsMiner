# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Pets and Children

**Đề bài:** While many families are keeping pets at home, some people fear that pets can harm children. What threats can pets cause to children? How to minimize those threats?

## Bài làm:

Nowadays, there are concerns about potential threats that pets may pose to children. While pets provide companionship and joy, it is important to recognize the risks they may present. In this essay, I will discuss the threats that pets can cause to children and suggest ways to minimize those risks.

Firstly, pets can pose physical threats to children while playing or interacting with them. For example, dogs or cats may scratch or bite children, especially when they feel threatened or are not properly trained. Secondly, some pets can carry diseases that can be transmitted to children, such as parasites or infections, that can lead to health issues especially in young children with weaker immune systems.

Fortunately, there are several ways to substantially minimize these threats by combining proper pet care and parental supervision. Parents bear the primary responsibility of ensuring their infants or toddlers are never left alone with pets. Parents can also educate their children on how to interact safely with pets, teach them to avoid sudden movements, and respect the animals' space. Equally important is the need for appropriate veterinary care for pets, including regular vaccinations and health check-ups. Many diseases or parasites can be detected and treated early before they pose a risk to children.

In conclusion, while pets can present certain threats to children, these risks can be effectively managed and reduced through responsible supervision, education, and suitable veterinary care. By taking these precautions, families can enjoy the benefits of having pets while ensuring the safety and well-being of their children.
